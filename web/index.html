    Good to know

    Buying your ticket online skips the ticket-booth line, not the boarding line. On summer weekends, boardings peak from noon to 3 pm; before 9:30 am or after 6 pm they run at under half that.

    How accurate is this?

    Live accuracy appears here once a few days of forecasts have been checked against the City's counts.

    How it works

    • The City of Toronto publishes how many ferry tickets are scanned at Jack Layton Ferry Terminal every 15 minutes. That is everyone heading to the Island, on all three routes combined. Trips back to the city need no ticket and are not counted.
    • A machine-learning model (gradient-boosted trees) learned from more than eleven years of those counts, alongside the weather, holidays, school breaks, how each holiday went in past years, and how ridership has been trending year over year.
    • For the next 7 days it uses live weather forecasts, averaging the models that were most accurate near the terminal. Months ahead, it shows what is typical for the date.
    • "Busy" means compared with what the scheduled boats can carry in that hour. The same 300 people is quiet on a summer afternoon and busy on a winter evening, when only the Ward's Island boat runs.
    • In winter only the Ward's Island route runs. Centre Island and Hanlan's Point are served from mid-April to mid-October.
